What we offer

Coaching Programs

Structured development pathways for every age and level

Ages 4–15

Junior Academy

Where champions begin. Our junior programme follows the ITF Play + Stay pathway to build technique, court movement, and a lifelong love of the sport. Age-grouped sessions run across the week in a fun, structured environment designed for real progress.

  • Red, Orange & Green ball stages (ITF Play + Stay pathway)
  • Small groups — maximum 6 per coach for focused attention
  • School holiday camps and weekend intensives
  • Internal SMTL leagues and inter-academy matchplay
  • Regular parent updates and written progress reports
Competitive Players

Performance Elite

Designed for players who compete — or want to. A high-intensity environment focused on technical refinement, tactical intelligence, physical conditioning, and the mental edge that separates good from great. Tournament preparation and real match play are at the core of everything.

  • Advanced stroke mechanics and video analysis
  • Tactical pattern drilling and match strategy sessions
  • On-court fitness, speed and agility training
  • SMTL 1000 & 500 league participation with live rankings
  • UAE open tournament preparation and college placement support
All Adult Levels

Adult Tennis Program

Never picked up a racquet? Played for years and want to sharpen your game? Our adult program meets you where you are — relaxed, encouraging, and genuinely effective.

  • Beginner through to advanced groups
  • Morning, evening and weekend slots
  • Social match play and club leagues
  • Private and semi-private lessons available
  • Cardio Tennis fitness classes

Beyond the Court

Other Services

Everything you need to keep your game — and your gear — in top shape.

Racket Care

Stringing Services

Keep your racket performing at its best. Our in-house stringing service uses premium strings across a range of gauges and tensions, set up to match your playing style — from control-focused setups to high-powered strings for aggressive baseliners.

  • Wide range of string types and gauges
  • Custom tension setup to match your game
  • Fast turnaround — most rackets ready within 24–48 hours
  • Available to members and non-members
Gear Up

Racquet Rentals

Don't own a racquet yet, or want to try before you buy? Rent a quality racquet for your session, lesson or casual match — perfect for beginners, guests and visiting players.

  • Range of racquets across multiple brands and levels
  • Daily and weekly rental options
  • Ideal for beginners, guests and visiting players
  • Available to members and non-members
Practice Smarter

Ball Machine Rental

Get in extra reps on your own schedule. Rent a ball machine for solo practice sessions — dial in your groundstrokes, volleys and serve returns at your own pace.

  • Adjustable speed, spin and feed rate
  • Great for solo practice and drilling
  • Hourly and session-based rental options
  • Available to members and non-members

Need a restring or a racquet?

Drop your racket off at Al Nasr Sports Centre, or message us to arrange stringing or a rental.
